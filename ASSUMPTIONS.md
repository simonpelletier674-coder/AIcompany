# ASSUMPTIONS — the living register of open bets

Every important uncertainty gets a row: the current bet, the evidence behind it,
the test that will settle it, and the trigger to revisit. Reviewed on metric ticks.
This exists because the operator is right: pricing, clarity, positioning are
questions to keep asking while building — not decisions to make once.

## A1 — Pricing ($29 Solo / $49 Pro)
- **Bet:** launch low; the binding constraint at zero brand is TRUST, not price.
- **Evidence:** Castmagic $39–299/mo and Podsqueeze $12–149/mo bracket the band.
  Value anchor is strong: human LinkedIn ghostwriters run $500–2,000+/mo, so even
  $49 is ~10x under the human alternative. Margin safe at any price (COGS $1–2/mo).
- **Suspicion:** probably UNDERPRICED for the business-development buyer (one client
  win pays for years of it). $29 may attract hobbyists rather than the ICP.
- **Test:** after the first 10 paying customers, raise list price to $39/$79 with
  founding members grandfathered at launch pricing; measure conversion drop.
- **Revisit trigger:** first 10 customers, or if trial→paid conversion > 40%
  (signal we're leaving money on the table).

## A2 — Landing clarity
- **Bet:** hero must state the deliverable, not just the outcome.
- **Evidence:** naive-reader test 2026-08-22: hero 5/10 ("a week of content" too
  abstract), whole page 7/10. Fixes applied: sub now says "five ready-to-post
  LinkedIn drafts plus guest follow-up emails"; pricing header now does the math
  ("about $7 a week").
- **Test:** re-run naive-reader test after any copy change; once traffic exists,
  the real metric is visitor→waitlist conversion (healthy benchmark ~10–25% for
  targeted traffic to an early-access page).
- **Revisit trigger:** conversion < 5% on ≥200 targeted visitors.

## A3 — Output quality proof ("would I rewrite them all?")
- **Bet:** the HBR demo proves capability, but skeptics still ask if drafts are
  postable AS-IS. The strongest future proof: a real user's post published
  unchanged, with engagement shown.
- **Test:** first 3 trial users → ask permission to feature one unedited
  posted-as-is example. Also queued: regenerate demo from a SOLO-host show
  (closer to ICP than HBR).
- **Revisit trigger:** first trial user onboarded.

## A4 — ICP (solo B2B hosts podcasting for business development)
- **Bet:** consultants/coaches/founders/agency owners; NOT hobby podcasters, NOT
  download-maximizers.
- **Test:** waitlist signup sources + domains (business emails vs gmail) + which
  launch posts convert. Adjust persona cards and channels accordingly.
- **Revisit trigger:** 30 waitlist signups (analyze composition).

## A5 — Channel (organic communities + SEO before any paid)
- **Bet:** podcaster communities and "turn podcast into LinkedIn posts" search
  intent are reachable without spend; guest-side virality (quotable follow-ups)
  is the compounding loop.
- **Test:** track per-channel signups via ref field already stored in D1.
- **Revisit trigger:** 4 weeks of distribution (the kill-criteria checkpoint).

## A6 — Email-first product, dashboard-second
- **Bet:** delivery via inbox is the right MVP; dashboard ships with billing.
- **Test:** ask first trial users what they miss; watch whether "where do I see
  my show memory?" comes up unprompted.
- **Revisit trigger:** first 5 trial users.
