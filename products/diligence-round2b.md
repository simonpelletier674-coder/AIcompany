# Due Diligence: ComplianceJobsRadar, AI Governance Directory, LicenseTrack — all KILLED

Date: 2026-08-22. Same adversarial process as diligence-round2.md.

## ComplianceJobsRadar — KILL
- Comparables wrong-shape (L2): WeWorkRemotely/Dynamite Jobs are generalist boards
  selling access to 15 years of accumulated audience; employers pay for traffic, not
  listings. Dynamite Jobs is $399, not $249–449 as claimed.
- The niche is owned by the most authoritative possible incumbent: ACAMS (the
  certifying body itself) runs careers.acams.org. SCCE compliance job board charges
  $500/90d and sells "25,000 opted-in professionals" — audience again.
- Also crowded: ComplianceJobs.io/.org, ComplianceCrossing. Not white space.
- Seeding supply by scraping Indeed/LinkedIn is ToS-prohibited; scraped-free listings
  next to $299 paid ones undercut the paid pitch anyway.
- Cold-start against ACAMS with $100/mo: year-plus, if ever. FATAL.

## AI Governance & Compliance Software Directory — KILL
- G2 $2,999/yr (jumps to $6k yr 2) and Capterra $500/mo PPC are traffic monetization
  — non-transferable to a zero-traffic site (L2).
- "AI governance software" is a contested 2025–26 gold-rush keyword: G2 category page,
  vendor content teams, and Google AI Overviews already occupy it. Zero-DA site has
  no path to page one in 6–18 months.
- Free authoritative alternative: OECD.ai tools catalogue (L1).
- Thin vendor TAM (~16 funded startups/yr) reachable only via outbound B2B sales —
  a motion this company cannot run. Category label churn risk on top. FATAL.

## LicenseTrack (funeral director CE tracker) — KILL
- TAM ~24.5–32k licensees; realistic capture = low hundreds of users.
- Cadence broken (L4): renewals are BIENNIAL with 6–20 CE hrs per 2 years — monthly
  billing on a once-every-two-years event. CredTally (closest comp) is FREE at this
  usage level (≤2 credentials); Harbor Compliance is enterprise B2B (L2 twice).
- NFDA already emails CE reminders; state boards send renewal notices (L1-adjacent).
- Audience is offline/association-bound — no organic surface to land on.
- Real liability if CE data is wrong (board discipline). The only viable pivot
  (chains, annual pricing) requires outbound B2B — ruled out by constraints.
- Strongest of the three (genuine white space; failure is parametric not structural)
  but still KILL under this company's constraints.

## PATTERN AUTOPSY — all five kills (this file + diligence-round2.md)
Common failure modes:
1. Thin repackaging of information an authoritative source publishes free (L1, x5).
2. Monthly billing bolted onto infrequent/periodic events (L4, x3).
3. Two-sided cold-start economics (boards/directories worthless without audience).
4. Wrong comparables treated as evidence — different buyer type or product shape (L2, x4).
5. Audiences the company structurally cannot reach (procurement, offline professions,
   outbound-B2B-only, scam-trained recipients) (L3).

## The shape that avoids all five (adopted as round-3 thesis)
- The AI's LABOR IS THE PRODUCT: continuous individualized work on the BUYER'S OWN
  data/content — transforming, generating, computing — not re-surfacing public facts.
- Single-sided and self-serve: one paying user gets full value on day one; no
  network effects, no marketplaces, no directories.
- Billing cadence = usage cadence: price what genuinely recurs weekly/monthly.
- Buyer: prosumer/freelancer/small-business, reachable via search + $100/mo, who
  self-checkouts on Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy. No procurement, no sales calls.
- No regulated-advice liability surface (no legal/medical/licensing correctness
  promises).
- Comparables pressure-tested for buyer type + product shape BEFORE being believed.
