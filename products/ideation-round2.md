## 1. TrademarkPulse — renewal & watch alerts for solo trademark owners

**Pitch:** A $79–99/year (per mark) USPTO monitoring service for solopreneurs, Etsy/Shopify brand owners, and indie founders who self-filed a trademark (TEAS Plus, LegalZoom) and have no law firm on retainer to track Section 8/15 renewal deadlines or watch for confusingly-similar new filings.

**Target buyer & pain:** A DIY trademark filer risks losing the mark entirely if they miss a renewal deadline (5–6 years out, then every 10) or fails to notice a squatter filing something similar. Low-frequency, high-consequence deadline nobody remembers to track.

**Maintained-asset moat:** Continuously updated USPTO TSDR/TESS feed cross-referenced against each customer's mark, deadline logic re-run weekly, new-filing similarity checks daily. A copycat starting today has zero deadline-tracking history and no accumulated similarity-alert corpus.

**Monetization:** $9/mo or $79/yr per mark, Gumroad subscription; volume discount for agencies managing multiple client marks.

**Payment evidence:** Commercial trademark watch services price per-mark monitoring from $99/yr (budget tier) up to $490/yr (50-state) and $3,000/yr (federal-only), with enterprise portfolio services at $300–2,000/month. Sources: tramatm.com trademark watch cost Q&A; housecar.life trademark watch services review 2026.

**First distribution channel:** r/Entrepreneur, r/smallbusiness, Etsy/Shopify seller groups where "I forgot to renew my trademark" horror stories circulate; SEO for "USPTO trademark renewal deadline."

**Scores:** WTP 5(x2=10) · Advantage fit 4(x2=8) · Buildable 4 · CF fit 5 · Organic 4 · Autonomous 5 → **36/40**

## 2. EPR Compliance Radar — state packaging law tracker for small CPG brands

**Pitch:** $79–149/mo dashboard + alert feed tracking Extended Producer Responsibility packaging law deadlines, fees, and registration rules across the 7+ enacted states (CA, CO, ME, MD, MN, OR, WA), for the ops person at a 10–200 person CPG brand with no law-firm retainer.

**Maintained-asset moat:** Only useful if re-verified against each state PRO's site on a rolling basis; brand-new fast-changing regulatory area. Copycat starts with zero historical deadline/fee-change log.

**Payment evidence:** Major law firms (Proskauer, Mayer Brown) publish paid-advisory content on exactly this; dedicated EPR reporting software described as critical infrastructure. Only tracker found (EPR Atlas) is free and basic → room for paid alerting tier.

**Scores:** WTP 3(x2=6) · Advantage fit 5(x2=10) · Buildable 3 · CF fit 5 · Organic 4 · Autonomous 4 → **32/40**

## 3. HardwareGrantRadar — SBIR/STTR + state manufacturing grant digest for hard-tech founders

**Pitch:** $49/mo curated, continuously updated feed of SBIR/STTR solicitations, state manufacturing grants, and DOE/DOD hard-tech funding deadlines, filtered for hardware/manufacturing startups.

**Target buyer & pain:** Hardware founders lack grant-writing staff and miss funding windows across dozens of agency sites; a missed SBIR deadline is a missed six-figure non-dilutive funding cycle.

**Maintained-asset moat:** Raw data is public, but a filtered, deduplicated, hardware-specific feed with historical deadline patterns and agency notes accumulates; copycat has no history of which agencies/topics repeat annually.

**Monetization:** $49/mo digest + deadline calendar; $99/mo tier adds proposal-template library.

**Payment evidence:** GrantWatch charges $18/week–$199/year; Instrumentl charges $299–$999/month for curated grant databases — grant-seekers demonstrably pay recurring money for maintained filtered feeds over free Grants.gov alerts.

**First distribution channel:** Hacker News Show HN, Hardware Founders Slack, hardware-focused accelerator alumni channels.

**Scores:** WTP 5(x2=10) · Advantage fit 4(x2=8) · Buildable 4 · CF fit 5 · Organic 4 · Autonomous 5 → **36/40**
