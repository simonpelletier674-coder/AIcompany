## Top 3 detail

### 2. Podcast → LinkedIn Ghostwriter with Guest Continuity (37/40)
$29–49/mo. Solo B2B/expert podcast hosts. Ingests RSS/audio weekly; maintains a
growing episode+guest knowledge base; produces a week of LinkedIn posts per episode
that cross-reference prior episodes, plus drafted guest follow-up emails. Chatbot
can do one episode; it cannot remember the other 40. Marginal cost ~$1–2/customer/mo
(~95% margin). Comparable: Castmagic $39–299/mo, Podsqueeze $12–149/mo (same buyer,
same shape — podcasters already pay monthly for AI transformation of their own
audio). Wedge: neither targets host personal-brand LinkedIn ghostwriting nor builds
persistent cross-episode guest-relationship memory. Distribution: podcaster
communities, guest-swap virality (hosts show it to guests).

### 4. Consultant Cross-Session Client Continuity Briefs (35/40)
$49–79/mo. Freelance consultants/coaches with 5–15 retainer clients. Every call
folds into per-client persistent memory; before the next call, a prep brief: open
commitments, unresolved threads, promises made. Fireflies/Fathom summarize the past
one call at a time; this prepares the future from the whole history. Marginal cost
$3–6/mo (~90% margin). Comparable: Fireflies.ai Pro $18/mo, huge self-serve base of
consultants paying to process their own call data. Scope strictly business
consulting/coaching (no legal/medical/therapy) for liability. Distribution: LinkedIn,
consultant communities.

### 8. Newsletter Voice-Continuity Repurposing (35/40)
$29–49/mo. Substack/Beehiiv writers. Ingests each issue + full archive; produces
platform-native repurposed content (X threads, LinkedIn, carousel copy) consistent
with the writer's voice and non-repetitive vs the archive; pushes drafts to
scheduling tools. Marginal cost <$1/mo (~96% margin). Comparable: Repurpose.io
$35–79/mo (same shape/buyer — creators paying monthly for continuous repurposing of
their own content); it is video/clip-first, leaving text-first archive-aware voice
matching unclaimed. Distribution: newsletter-creator communities, cross-promo swaps.

## Generator's bet
Podcast→LinkedIn first (proven $30–300/mo payment in the exact shape via
Castmagic/Podsqueeze; unclaimed continuity wedge); consultant briefs fast-follow;
newsletter repurposing in reserve as lowest-risk build.
