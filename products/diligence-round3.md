# Due Diligence: Round-3 Top Three — one survivor

Date: 2026-08-22. Kill-test calibrated with L10 (competition = demand evidence).

## Idea 1: Podcast → LinkedIn ghostwriter — PROCEED WITH CHANGES ✅
- Original wedge framing FALSE: Castmagic already ships Speaker Insights (per-guest
  profiles across the library) and LinkedIn post outputs; Podsqueeze does LinkedIn
  summaries. The idea survives; the pitch didn't.
- LinkedIn posting path VIABLE: w_member_social (post to own profile via 3-legged
  OAuth) is an open, no-review permission — real auto-posting possible without
  partner approval. (Caution: automated-looking activity draws scrutiny; pilot small.)
- TAM: ~249k "Business" podcasts, but only ~30.5k active in last 90 days (~12%);
  podfade is severe → design for pause-not-cancel, market only to active shows.
- Unit economics fine ($0.36/hr transcription; trivial vs $29–49/mo).
- ChatGPT Projects: partial pressure only (~8k-token project memory, no automation
  pipeline) — price-ceiling effect, not collapse.
- REQUIRED CHANGES: (1) reposition from content-volume to GUEST-RELATIONSHIP angle —
  LinkedIn copy that calls back to what a guest said when they reappear/get
  referenced + CRM-lite guest follow-up drafts tied to open threads; a relationship/
  sales product, not another repurposer. (2) Pause-not-cancel billing; acquire only
  RSS-fresh shows. (3) Drafts by default; auto-post as piloted opt-in.

## Idea 2: Consultant continuity briefs — KILL ❌
- FATAL: Granola and Read AI already ship proactive pre-meeting briefs (open
  threads, context, agenda) — the exact differentiator, from funded incumbents who
  own the meeting-recording relationship.
- Stacked: meeting-bot infra needs Zoom Marketplace review (weeks, hard for a
  no-name); manual re-upload friction contradicts the "invisible continuity" value;
  11–14 all-party-consent states = liability on a brandless no-support operator;
  ChatGPT-Projects-per-client approximates the value.

## Idea 3: Newsletter voice repurposing — WEAK SURVIVOR (reserve) ⚠️
- Supergrow already does voice-matched repurposing of newsletters/blogs/PDFs into
  LinkedIn content; ContentIn/Taplio/Typefully/Hypefury crowd the $8–199/mo band.
- Buffer API not usable for third-party publishing today (legacy closed, GraphQL
  beta personal-keys only) — the integration as specified is unbuildable.
- No verified evidence newsletter writers pay for repurposing specifically.
- Kept as reserve only.
